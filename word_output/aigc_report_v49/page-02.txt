摘要：骨干网络源—目的节点对流量兼有短时起伏和较慢趋势，单一时间分辨率难以同时表征两者。本文采用
尺度1、2和4的DLinear专家，由轻量路由网络根据输入窗口统计量生成Softmax融合权重。在输入长度96、预

Abstract: Short-term fluctuations and slower trends coexist in backbone origin-destination traff
ic. Input-window statistics determine the Softmax weights used to combine DLinear experts at sca
les 1, 2, and 4. For input length 96 and forecast length 24, results from three common random se
eds give MSE reductions of 4.07% on Abilene and 4.54% on GÉANT relative to DLinear. The correspo
nding reductions relative to LightTS are 29.80% and 46.89%. An eight-seed ablation adds two fixe

nd 4.97%, respectively. Both paired 95% confidence intervals on GÉANT include zero, and most of 
its pooled router-weight variance arises between seeds. Reliable window-specific scale selection
 therefore remains unconfirmed despite the average error reduction.
