### 1. 中文摘要

**修改前：**

摘要：为提高小时级骨干网络流量的多步预测精度，构建了轻量自适应多尺度预测模型。模型将历史窗口转换为三种时间分辨率的序列，分别交由独立的分解线性专家处理，以兼顾局部变化和较慢趋势。路由网络提取窗口的总体水平、离散程度、末时状态和平均变化强度，经两层感知器生成融合权重，得到未来多步预测。在输入长度为96、预测长度为24的Abilene与GÉANT实验中，三个共同随机种子下的均方误差相对单尺度模型分别降低4.07%和4.54%，相对采样导向轻量模型分别降低29.80%和46.89%。八种子消融进一步比较全局可学习权重与恒定输入路由，前一数据集的平均改进率均约1.80%，后一数据集分别为4.31%和4.97%。结果表明，该结构在两套骨干网数据上取得了较低的平均预测误差，低维路由仅增加少量参数，可为未来一天的需求观察与容量规划提供预测参考。

**修改后：**

### 2. 英文摘要

**修改前：**

Abstract: A lightweight adaptive multiscale model was developed to improve multistep forecasting of hourly backbone traffic. Historical windows were represented at three temporal resolutions. Independent decomposition linear predictors captured local changes and slower trends. A two-layer perceptron used four window statistics to produce normalized fusion weights. Experiments on Abilene and GÉANT used an input length of 96 and a forecast length of 24. Across three common random seeds, mean squared error decreased by 4.07% and 4.54% relative to the single-scale model, and by 29.80% and 46.89% relative to the sampling-oriented lightweight model. Eight-seed ablations included learned global weights and a constant-input router. Mean seed-wise improvements over these controls were both approximately 1.80% on Abilene, and 4.31% and 4.97% on GÉANT, respectively. The model achieves lower mean errors on both backbone datasets with limited additional routing parameters. It provides forecasting information for next-day demand observation and capacity planning.

**修改后：**

Abstract: A lightweight adaptive multiscale model was developed to improve multistep forecasting of hourly backbone traffic. Historical windows were represented at three temporal resolutions, and independent decomposition linear (DLinear) predictors generated candidate forecasts. A two-layer perceptron used four window statistics to calculate fusion weights: overall level, dispersion, final-time state and mean absolute change. Experiments on Abilene and GÉANT used an input length of 96 and a forecast length of 24. Across three common random seeds, mean squared error decreased by 4.07% and 4.54% relative to single-scale DLinear, respectively. The corresponding reductions relative to the sampling-oriented lightweight model LightTS were 29.80% and 46.89%. Eight-seed ablations compared the model with learned global weights and a constant-input router. Mean seed-wise improvements over both controls were approximately 1.80% on Abilene. On GÉANT, the improvements over learned global weights and the constant-input router were 4.31% and 4.97%, respectively. Combining multiscale forecasting with low-dimensional routing achieves lower mean errors with few additional fusion parameters. The forecasts provide a reference for next-day network demand analysis and capacity planning.

**修改原因：** 与中文摘要逐项对应，明确DLinear与LightTS；工作和结果用过去时，结论用现在时。

### 5. 引言第4段

**修改前：**

常见流程先给定尺度，再按固定比例融合预测。输入窗口的短时波动和平缓趋势占比不同，单尺度难以覆盖多分辨率信息，固定融合也未使用窗口状态差异。本文据此计算输入相关权重，组合各尺度预测。

**修改后：**

对于同一历史窗口，不同时间分辨率保留的信息并不相同。将各尺度预测按固定比例融合，可以利用多尺度信息，但融合比例不会随输入状态变化。因此，本文研究在保持尺度专家结构一致的条件下，依据窗口状态生成融合权重是否能够进一步降低整体预测误差。

**修改原因：** 把对“常见流程”的笼统判断改为明确的融合设计问题，不将既有多尺度方法一概描述为固定权重。
