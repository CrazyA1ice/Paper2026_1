# Paired weekly advantage figure

Question: are the reported GÉANT 168-hour improvements consistent across repeated training?
Claim: all eight matched seeds have lower MSE and MAE than each of the four controls.
Design: quantitative grid, two aligned panels. Each row is a control, each small point is one matched seed; diamonds and horizontal bars are mean and sample SD, not confidence intervals. A zero-effect reference and every seed are shown. Positive values mean lower error for the proposed method. No p-values.
Scope: the already reported weekly task, all five models and all 307 test origins. The complete horizon grid has a different question and is not mixed into this figure. No results or numerical observations changed.
Journal: retained 138 mm width and 60.96 mm image height, 7.5 pt Songti/Times, 600 dpi; editable SVG/PDF and TIFF exports. Manuscript layout and image container geometry remain unchanged.
